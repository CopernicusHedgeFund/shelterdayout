Puppy adoption near you

Shelters and humane societies take in puppies year-round, with the most in spring and summer. Puppies at shelters are usually vaccinated, microchipped and spayed or neutered before they go home, and the adoption fee covers it. Puppies go fast, so check each shelter's adoptable animals page often.

Questions people ask

Where can I adopt a puppy near me?

From city and county animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs. Pick your state below; every shelter links to its own adoptable dogs page, where puppies are listed.

Is it cheaper to adopt a puppy from a shelter?

Usually. Shelter fees for puppies are often higher than for adult dogs, but they include spay or neuter, vaccines and a microchip, which cost far more separately.

How do I get a puppy before someone else does?

Check the shelter's site daily, apply early (many shelters let you apply before you visit), and watch for adoption events.
