Animal control and county animal shelters near you

Animal control is run by a city, county or parish. It takes in stray and surrendered animals, and most animal control shelters also adopt pets out, often for lower fees than private shelters. For emergencies involving a dangerous animal, call 911 or your local non-emergency police number.

Questions people ask

Can you adopt from animal control?

Yes, most city and county animal services shelters adopt out dogs and cats. Each one below links to its own site.

What does animal control do?

Animal control picks up strays, investigates bites and cruelty reports, enforces local pet laws and runs the public shelter in many areas.

My pet is lost. Where should I look?

Check your county or city animal shelter first, in person if you can, plus nearby humane societies. Shelters keep strays only a few days before they can be adopted out.
