Fee-waived and reduced-fee adoption events

Shelters often waive or cut adoption fees when they're full, around holidays, and during national events like Clear the Shelters. Here are the upcoming ones we've found on shelters' own websites.

  • Sun, Nov 1, 2026 · Stanislaus Animal Services Agency (Modesto, CA): Stars, Stripes & Second Chances: free adoptions for veterans, $11 dog and cat adoptions for everyone else
  • Thu, Nov 26, 2026 · Best Friends Pet Adoption Center - Los Angeles (formerly NKLA) (Los Angeles, CA): $15 Adoptions (final day of Nov 9-26 promotion)
  • Fri, Nov 27, 2026 · Santa Barbara Humane - Santa Barbara Campus (Santa Barbara, CA): Adoption fees waived for most animals (excluding VIPs) through Nov 30
  • Fri, Nov 27, 2026 · Santa Barbara Humane - Santa Maria Campus (Santa Maria, CA): Adoption fees waived for most animals (excluding VIPs) through Nov 30
  • Sat, Nov 28, 2026 · Pet Circle Regional Animal Center (Waco, TX): Half Price Adoptions Every Weekend (recurring weekends)
  • Fri, Dec 4, 2026 · Pet Circle Regional Animal Center (Waco, TX): First Friday FREE Adoptions
  • Fri, Dec 11, 2026 · Houston Humane (Houston, TX): Chewy Claus Holiday Pet Adoption Special (free adoptions)
  • Sat, Dec 12, 2026 · Humane Society of Tulsa (Tulsa, OK): Adoptions at Hollywood Feed

Reduced fees don't mean a shortcut on the process: shelters still ask for an application and a meet-and-greet. See our adoption checklist.
