Kitten adoption near you

⁠​‍​​​​‍⁢​‌⁢‌​⁢‌‌⁠Kitten season runs from spring through fall, when shelters are full of kittens needing homes. Many shelters offer two-kitten discounts, since kittens adopted in pairs keep each other company and learn good manners from each other.

Questions people ask

Where can I adopt a kitten near me?

From animal shelters, humane societies, SPCAs and cat rescues with adoption centers. Choose your state below and open the shelter's adoptable cats page.

How old does a kitten have to be to be adopted?

Shelters usually adopt out kittens at about 8 weeks old and 2 pounds, once they're old enough to be spayed or neutered.

Should I adopt two kittens?

Many shelters encourage it and some discount the second kitten. Two kittens play with each other and are often easier than one.
