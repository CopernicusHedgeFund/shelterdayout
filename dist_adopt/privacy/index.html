Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

ShelterAdoption is a free program of Charity Good (charitygood.org), a nonprofit. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

You can browse ShelterAdoption without an account and without giving us any personal information. The site has no forms of its own. Suggestions, corrections and listing claims are sent through our sister site ShelterDayOut and handled under its privacy policy.

Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and troubleshooting. To count visits we use our own cookie-free counter: it records the page, the site you came from, your device type and browser family, and a daily anonymous code that can't be traced back to you. It stores no IP address and sets no cookies. The site sets no tracking cookies and uses no advertising trackers.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address under Google's privacy policy. Links to shelters and maps take you to other sites with their own policies.

How we use and share it

We use what you send only to respond to you, keep listings accurate and run the site. We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Shelter contact details

Listings show organizations' public business contact details (website, main phone and public email) as published on their own websites or government pages. To correct or remove a listing's details, email hello@shelterdayout.com.

Keeping and deleting

We keep form submissions for up to 24 months and server logs for as long as our host keeps them, then delete them. To see, correct or delete what you sent us, email hello@shelterdayout.com and we'll respond within 30 days, whatever state you live in.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, and form data is stored outside the public website. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We'll post any changes here with a new date. Questions: hello@shelterdayout.com. Mail: Charity Good, 121 South Tejon Street, Suite 900, Colorado Springs, CO 80903.

ShelterAdoption is a free directory of animal shelters. We don't place animals or handle adoptions; each shelter runs its own adoption process. Listings are checked monthly, but details change, so confirm with the shelter before you go.

Not ready to adopt? ShelterDayOut, our sister site, lists shelters that let you take a dog out for the day.
