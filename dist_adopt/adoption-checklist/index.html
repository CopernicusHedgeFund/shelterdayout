Pet adoption checklist

Print this or keep it on your phone.

Bring to the shelter

  • Photo ID
  • Landlord's name and number, or proof you can have pets, if you rent
  • Your current pets' vet records, if the shelter asks
  • A leash and collar (dogs) or a carrier (cats)
  • Everyone in your household, if the shelter wants to meet them

Questions to ask

  • Why was this animal surrendered or found?
  • How do they do with kids, dogs and cats?
  • Any health issues or medications?
  • What vaccines and treatment have they had?
  • What does the fee include? Is there a return policy?

Have ready at home

  • Food (ask what the shelter feeds, and switch slowly)
  • Food and water bowls
  • Bed or crate (dogs); litter box, litter and scratching post (cats)
  • ID tag with your phone number
  • A few toys and chews
  • A local vet picked out (find a vet near you)

ShelterAdoption is a free directory of animal shelters. We don't place animals or handle adoptions; each shelter runs its own adoption process. Listings are checked monthly, but details change, so confirm with the shelter before you go.

Not ready to adopt? ShelterDayOut, our sister site, lists shelters that let you take a dog out for the day.
