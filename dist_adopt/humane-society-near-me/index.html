Humane societies and SPCAs near you

Humane societies and SPCAs are usually independent local nonprofits, despite the shared names: the Humane Society of the United States and the ASPCA don't run local shelters. Most local humane societies run an adoption center, low-cost vet care and volunteer programs.

Questions people ask

Is the humane society the same as the SPCA?

No. Both names are used by many separate local nonprofits. Each one is run on its own, with its own fees and rules.

Is the humane society the same as animal control?

Usually not. Animal control is a city or county service that takes in strays; humane societies are nonprofits, though some run the local shelter under contract.

How do I adopt from the humane society?

Look at the adoptable animals on its website, then visit or apply. Bring a photo ID; the fee usually covers spay or neuter, vaccines and a microchip.
