Questions about adopting in Georgia

How many animal shelters are in Georgia?

We list 46 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Georgia, in 41 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Georgia?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 10 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Georgia?

None are listed right now. Check back soon, or see adoption events nationwide on our events page.
