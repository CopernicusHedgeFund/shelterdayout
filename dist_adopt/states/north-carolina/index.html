We list 76 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in North Carolina, in 71 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in North Carolina?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 12 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.
