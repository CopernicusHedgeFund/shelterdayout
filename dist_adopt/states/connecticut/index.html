Questions about adopting in Connecticut

How many animal shelters are in Connecticut?

We list 32 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Connecticut, in 31 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Connecticut?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 3 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Connecticut?

None are listed right now. Check back soon, or see adoption events nationwide on our events page.
