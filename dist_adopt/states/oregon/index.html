Questions about adopting in Oregon

How many animal shelters are in Oregon?

We list 39 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Oregon, in 30 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Oregon?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 13 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Oregon?

None are listed right now. Check back soon, or see adoption events nationwide on our events page.

ShelterAdoption is a free directory of animal shelters. We don't place animals or handle adoptions; each shelter runs its own adoption process. Listings are checked monthly, but details change, so confirm with the shelter before you go.

Not ready to adopt? ShelterDayOut, our sister site, lists shelters that let you take a dog out for the day.
