Questions about adopting in Washington

How many animal shelters are in Washington?

We list 71 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Washington, in 57 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Washington?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 21 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Washington?

Yes: 5 upcoming adoption events are listed above, taken from the shelters' own websites.

ShelterAdoption is a free directory of animal shelters. We don't place animals or handle adoptions; each shelter runs its own adoption process. Listings are checked monthly, but details change, so confirm with the shelter before you go.

Not ready to adopt? ShelterDayOut, our sister site, lists shelters that let you take a dog out for the day.
