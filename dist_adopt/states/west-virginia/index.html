Questions about adopting in West Virginia

How many animal shelters are in West Virginia?

We list 50 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in West Virginia, in 47 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in West Virginia?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 19 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.
