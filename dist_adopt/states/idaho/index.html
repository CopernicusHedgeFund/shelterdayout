Questions about adopting in Idaho

How many animal shelters are in Idaho?

We list 30 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Idaho, in 28 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Idaho?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 7 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Idaho?

None are listed right now. Check back soon, or see adoption events nationwide on our events page.
