Questions about adopting in Mississippi

How many animal shelters are in Mississippi?

We list 31 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Mississippi, in 27 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Mississippi?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 8 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.
