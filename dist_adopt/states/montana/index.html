Questions about adopting in Montana

How many animal shelters are in Montana?

We list 25 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Montana, in 21 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Montana?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 5 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.
