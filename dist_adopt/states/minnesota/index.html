Questions about adopting in Minnesota

How many animal shelters are in Minnesota?

We list 22 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Minnesota, in 21 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Minnesota?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 8 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.
