Questions about adopting in Massachusetts

How many animal shelters are in Massachusetts?

We list 72 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Massachusetts, in 62 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Massachusetts?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 13 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Massachusetts?

Yes: 9 upcoming adoption events are listed above, taken from the shelters' own websites.
