Questions about adopting in Missouri

How many animal shelters are in Missouri?

We list 90 animal shelters, humane societies and SPCAs in Missouri, in 81 cities and towns. The list is checked every month.

Where can I adopt a dog or cat in Missouri?

At any shelter below. Each one links to its own adoptable animals page, which it updates daily. 22 of them are humane societies or SPCAs.

Are there adoption events in Missouri?

None are listed right now. Check back soon, or see adoption events nationwide on our events page.
