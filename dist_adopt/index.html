    Questions people ask

    How do I adopt a pet from a shelter near me?

    Find a shelter below, look at its adoptable animals on its own website, then visit or apply. Most shelters ask for a photo ID, a short application and a meet-and-greet. See how to adopt a dog and how to adopt a cat.

    Where can I see the animals available for adoption?

    On each shelter's own website. Shelters update their adoptable animals daily, so we link straight to them rather than copying listings that go out of date.

    How much does it cost to adopt from a shelter?

    Fees vary by shelter, animal and age. They usually cover spay or neuter, vaccines and a microchip. Many shelters hold reduced-fee or fee-waived adoption events. See adoption fees.

    Are there adoption events near me?

    Yes. Our adoption events calendar lists upcoming adoption days, open houses and fee-waived events taken from shelters' own websites.

    Can I spend time with a shelter dog before adopting?

    Often, yes. Many shelters let you take a dog out for the day or host a sleepover. Our sister site ShelterDayOut lists those programs.

    Where can I find rescue dogs, puppies or kittens near me?

    City and county shelters, humane societies and SPCAs all have rescue dogs, puppies, cats and kittens. Search by your city above; each shelter's website shows who is available today.

    What's the difference between animal control, a humane society and an SPCA?

    Animal control is usually run by a city or county and takes in strays; many also adopt animals out. Humane societies and SPCAs are usually independent nonprofits, despite the shared names. All of them are listed here.

    Is ShelterAdoption free?

    Yes. It's a free directory run by the nonprofit Charity Good. We don't charge shelters or adopters and we don't handle adoptions.
